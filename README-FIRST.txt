UPLOAD ONLY index.html TO GITHUB.

1. Extract this ZIP.
2. In GitHub, click “uploading an existing file.”
3. Upload index.html.
4. Scroll down and click Commit changes.
5. Then open Settings > Pages.
6. Source: Deploy from a branch.
7. Branch: main. Folder: /(root).
8. Click Save.

The PayPal button is a placeholder until your payment link is added.
